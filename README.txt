Blasko's Pizza Food Safety Diary - updated

Updates:
- Add and save fridges in System; saved fridges appear in the Fridge diary dropdown.
- Fridge temperature records require a selected saved fridge.
- Time off can be blocked for a date range and cancelled from the calendar.
- LPG bottle readings calculate gas remaining automatically using 13.2 kg empty weight and 11 kg gas charge (24.2 kg full).
- Gas percentages are also saved with the diary entry.

This is still a browser/localStorage prototype. Data is stored on the device/browser used to enter it.
